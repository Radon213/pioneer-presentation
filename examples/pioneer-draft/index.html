Nonlinear Torsion Spring

Why the Series Elastic Element Is Nonlinear

SEA means series elastic actuator.

The spring lies in the torque path.

Store energy before delivering support.

Moon, thesis interim presentation, 2024-12-10: slides3–15 · original spring CAD · separate archived representative FEA04 / 6
Stage 0: SEA means series elastic actuator. The spring lies in the torque-transmission path between the motor-side drive and the output. Relative rotation deforms the spring and generates elastic torque. This gives the person a compliant interaction instead of an ideally rigid transmission. 한국어: 처음 듣는 청중을 위해 SEA의 위치와 역할을 먼저 정의한다. Stage 1: With a linear spring, low stiffness needs more relative deflection for the same torque, while high stiffness resists motion more strongly. This is a design trade-off rather than proof that every conventional SEA fails. We want yielding behavior at small loading and stronger torque transfer as deformation grows. 한국어: 낮은 강성과 큰 토크 전달 사이의 설계 상충을 설명한다. Stage 2: A nonlinear torque–deflection relationship is the mechanical objective. It changes how much added torque follows an added relative angle. This explains why a nonlinear spring is useful before showing the specific spiral geometry. No performance curve on this conceptual step is an experimental or learned result. 한국어: 목표 곡선의 개념과 검증된 특성곡선을 구분한다. Stage 3: Before turn contact, k1 << k2, so keq approximately follows k1. Once turn contact raises k1 well above k2, keq approximately follows k2 instead. The labels refer to tangent stiffness; k1 is the initially compliant lower unit and k2 is the upper unit. These are conceptual limits, not a fitted source-CAD law. In an ideal quasistatic serial pair, both springs transmit the same torque and their deflections add. The compliant unit deforms more initially; the stiffer unit also deforms, rather than waiting with zero torque. The axial source-CAD view exposes strip width and coil spacing; axial depth is a separate dimension. 한국어: 직렬 연결은 같은 토크이며 변위가 합쳐진다는 점을 분명히 한다. Stage 4: Identify strip width a, axial depth b and coil gap as separate dimensions. The native spring does not acquire nonlinearity from an invented variable-width profile. The historical concept uses turn approach and the winding limit to change additional compliance, with more added deflection then shared by the stiffer serial unit. This design illustration is not solved contact or a calibrated native spring law. Axial depth cannot be inferred from a top view alone. 한국어: 폭a·축방향 깊이b·코일 간격을 구분하고 실제 원본에 없는 가변 폭 형상을 주장하지 않는다. 다음 해석은 별도 대표형상이다.
