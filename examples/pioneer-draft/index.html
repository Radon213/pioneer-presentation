Nonlinear Spring

Why the Series Elastic Element Is Nonlinear

SEA means
series elastic actuator.

The spring lies in the torque path.

What should this spring allow?

Moon, thesis interim presentation, 2024-12-10: slides3–15 · original spring CAD · separate archived representative FEA04 / 8
Stage 0: SEA means series elastic actuator. The spring lies in the torque-transmission path between the motor-side drive and the output. Relative rotation deforms the spring and generates elastic torque. This gives the person a compliant interaction instead of an ideally rigid transmission. 한국어: 처음 듣는 청중을 위해 SEA의 위치와 역할을 먼저 정의한다. Stage 1: With a linear spring, low stiffness needs more relative deflection for the same torque, while high stiffness resists motion more strongly. This is a design trade-off rather than proof that every conventional SEA fails. We want yielding behavior at small loading and stronger torque transfer as deformation grows. 한국어: 낮은 강성과 큰 토크 전달 사이의 설계 상충을 설명한다. Stage 2: A nonlinear torque–deflection relationship is the mechanical objective. It changes how much added torque follows an added relative angle. This explains why a nonlinear spring is useful before showing the specific spiral geometry. No performance curve on this conceptual step is an experimental or learned result. 한국어: 목표 곡선의 개념과 검증된 특성곡선을 구분한다. Stage 3: In an ideal quasistatic serial pair, both springs transmit the same torque and their deflections add. The compliant unit deforms more initially; the stiffer unit also deforms, rather than waiting with zero torque. The axial source-CAD view exposes strip width and coil spacing; axial depth is a separate dimension. 한국어: 직렬 연결은 같은 토크이며 변위가 합쳐진다는 점을 분명히 한다. Stage 4: Identify strip width a, axial depth b and coil gap as separate dimensions. The native spring does not acquire nonlinearity from an invented variable-width profile. The historical concept uses turn approach and the winding limit to change additional compliance, with more added deflection then shared by the stiffer serial unit. This design illustration is not solved contact or a calibrated native spring law. Axial depth cannot be inferred from a top view alone. 한국어: 폭a·축방향 깊이b·코일 간격을 구분하고 실제 원본에 없는 가변 폭 형상을 주장하지 않는다. 다음 해석은 별도 대표형상이다. Stage 5: The actual FEA is a reconstructed two-arm representative geometry from the NAS, not the complete native compliant/stiff CAD assembly. Actual converged frames use circular inner-node motion and fixed outer attachments. The chosen full-envelope inter-arm contact model is frictionless and elastic. Displacements are shown at true scale. Source: assets/motion/spring-analysis-report.md ; assets/motion/spring-analysis-summary.json. 한국어: 실제 원본 스프링 시각화와 별도 대표 해석 형상을 명확히 분리한다. Stage 6: Original ANSYS CSV tables from the user-confirmed NAS backup are plotted with every finite torque point. P=2 mm, a=0.7 mm, b=10 mm spans 1.2–120 degrees; b=20 mm spans 0.45–45 degrees. Each panel has its own labelled axes. The original torque decrease is retained. These are archived prototype computations, not physical measurements or a calibrated law of the current weak/strong series pair. They are independent from the new 110-degree animation reconstruction; no point is synchronized to that animation. CSV headers, raw rows, exact paths and SHA256 are preserved in assets/motion/archived-spring-curves.json. 한국어: 보관된 원본 ANSYS 표를 그대로 표시하며 현재 직렬 스프링의 검증된 특성으로 주장하지 않는다.

Stage 0: For a referred motor angle and output angle, relative angle defines elastic deformation. A nonlinear spring maps that deformation to torque. The controller therefore needs to account for the spring state rather than requesting ideal joint assistance directly. 한국어: 모터 기준으로 환산된 각도와 출력 각도의 차이를 설명한다. Stage 1: Preload is a way to prepare an elastic state ahead of a later assistance demand. Amount and timing are coupled through motor limits, spring dynamics and the person’s movement. This is a control hypothesis to evaluate, not evidence of energy savings or an already optimized schedule. 한국어: 예압량과 시점을 제어 가설로 설명한다. Stage 2: The person changes movement in response to assistance, so a fixed demonstration waveform cannot settle the control problem. We need a coupled environment, consistent task conditions and valid comparisons before claiming that a strategy helps. 한국어: 시뮬레이션 환경의 필요성을 사람의 반응에서 도출한다.
